AI By Design Solutions

Q1. What are the firm's office hours?
A1. AI By Design Solutions operates Monday through Friday, from 8 AM to 5 PM.

Q2. What services does AI By Design Solutions offer?
A2. AI By Design Solutions provides consulting, implementation and technical support services for Cisco Unified Communications Manager, Cisco Unified Contact Center, Webex Calling, and Webex Contact Center.

Q3. Does the firm offer any warranties?
A3. Yes, the firm offers a 1 year, 3 year and 5 year warranty on any product we support. The warranty covers technical support and hardware replacements.

Q4. Can I schedule an appointment for service call?
A4. Yes, just ask to schedule an appointment with a technician and a support specialist can help you schedule one.

Q5. Does the firm support customers without a warranty?
A5. Customers without a warranty will be provided an hourly rate for support. We recommend talking to sales specialist first about available warranties to get a better rate.

Q6. Where is AI By Design Solutions located?
A6. Our office is located at 21 Street in London. Parking is available in the adjacent lot.

Q7. Can I request a specific technician for my appointment?
A7. While we try to accommodate preferences, availability may vary. Please inquire when scheduling your appointment.

Q8. How do I update my contact information?
A8. You can update your contact information by calling the sale team or visiting our website.

Q9. How are service calls billed for customers without a warranty?
A9. An invoice will be provided based on the hourly rate. Payment is due 30 days after the work is completed.

Q10. If I have a conflict with my service call can I reschedule?
A10. Service calls more than 24 hours out can be rescheduled at not cost. Service calls within 24 hours will have a $50 fee.

Q11. Can I access my service request, warranty details and billing online?
A11. Yes, customers who have a warranty with us can access the details of their contract, see their open and closed service requests and access billing info from our secure portal.

Q12. How do I become a new client with AI By Design Solutions?
A12. Contact our sales team to discuss the different warranty options and pricing.
